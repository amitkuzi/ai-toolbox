## Measurement criteria

### G3 (≥ 40% routed to T0/T1)

From routing suite and actual pilot:
- **Expected:** 50–70% of non-L3 decisions pick T0/T1
- **Test:** Run 15 test cases; measure tier distribution
- **Pass:** ≥ 40% actual; typically ≥ 50% if rules are well-tuned

### Consistency (T1 vs T2)

- **Same tier:** ≥ 65% (T1 and T2 agree exactly)
- **Adjacent tier:** ≤ 25% (T0↔T1, T1↔T2, T2↔T3 acceptable)
- **Divergent:** < 20% (T0↔T2, T0↔T3, T1↔T3; triggers ADR for V2 CLI)

### P4 compliance

- **Append-only:** Every line in diff is `+` (no `-` lines)
- **No validation errors:** `python scripts/validate.py` passes before and after
- **Line count:** Only increases (tools.jsonl, scores.jsonl, decisions.jsonl)
